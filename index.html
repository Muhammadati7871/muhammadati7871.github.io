ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

An Institution for Knowledge, Skills and Professional Growth

PN-HIMSAT is a higher education institution identified with the fields of management, science, arts and technology. The Institute aims to support students in developing academic knowledge, practical competence, responsible conduct and the skills needed to pursue further studies and professional opportunities.

Through academic learning and practical application, students are encouraged to build the foundation for lifelong learning and meaningful participation in society.

OUR APPROACH TO LEARNING

Learning Through Knowledge, Skills and Application

A competency-based approach places emphasis on the knowledge, skills, attitudes and abilities learners are expected to demonstrate. Where these methods are used, learning activities can encourage students to solve problems, communicate clearly, work with others and demonstrate what they have learned through appropriate assessment.
